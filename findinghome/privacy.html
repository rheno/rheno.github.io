Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

This policy covers the Finding Home game for iPhone and iPad ("the app"). In short: the app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves a small amount of game data only on your device:

  • which levels you have unlocked and which family members you have rescued,
  • your best star count for each level,
  • your Music and Sound on/off settings.

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. Choosing New game on the title screen resets your progress.

Network, analytics and advertising

The app works fully offline and makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
