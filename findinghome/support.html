Frequently asked questions

I can't hear any sound or music.

Finding Home follows your device's silent mode, so it can play alongside your own music. Turn off silent mode (the Ring/Silent switch or Action button) and turn up the volume. Also check that Music: On and Sound: On are set in the top-right corner of the title screen.

How do I play?

Use the ◀ ▶ buttons at the bottom left to run. You can slide your thumb between them. Tap JUMP to jump, and hold it to jump higher. Land on monsters to stomp them. Tap THROW to throw your wrench, which is the only way to beat Trash Chompers and Cone Crawlers. Bump gift blocks from below to get stars.

How do I double jump or glide?

Rescue Tom at the end of World 2 to unlock the double jump (tap JUMP again in the air). Rescue Lily at the end of World 3 to unlock the balloon glide (hold JUMP while falling).

Can I use a keyboard or game controller?

Yes. Keyboard: arrow keys or A/D to run, Space/W/↑ to jump, J or X to throw, P or Esc to pause. Controllers: stick or d-pad to run, A to jump, X or B to throw, Menu to pause.

What happens when I run out of lives?

You'll see GAME OVER. Choose Continue to get 3 lives back and restart the current world from its first level. Family members you've already rescued stay safe. You earn an extra life for every 30 stars.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Unlocked levels, rescued family members and your best stars are saved on your device. Your lives reset to 3 each time you open the app. To start over, choose New game on the title screen.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. The game plays in landscape.
