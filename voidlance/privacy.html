Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

This policy covers the Voidlance game for iPhone, iPad and Android ("the app"). In short: the app collects nothing itself. The only data handled is by Google AdMob, which shows the optional rewarded ads.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no servers of our own, no analytics and no save data that leaves your device. We do not know who you are or how you play.

Ads

The app offers optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob: when your ship is destroyed you can choose to watch one to earn an extra life. To serve these ads, Google's SDK may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), where you allow it,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • device and app information such as device model, OS version and app version,
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were completed.

Google handles this under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this data and do not sell it.

Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before requesting ads. You can change your answer later from the privacy settings button in the app.
  • iOS. The app asks permission before tracking. Change it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Ads are optional and only offered when you lose. Declining them never blocks the game.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
