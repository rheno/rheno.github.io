Need help?

Found a bug or have an idea? Email rhenobudiasa@outlook.com. Please include your device model and OS version, and expect a reply within a few days.

Common questions

I did not get a continue offer. The offer needs an internet connection and an ad that is ready to show. A run allows 3 continues. Without a ready ad the run simply restarts.

Can I change my ad consent? Yes, from the privacy settings button in the game, or on iOS under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
